# Body assets

- `idle-neutral.vrma`: her standing idle, from the 100STYLE dataset's Neutral idle take (frames 474 to 954), retargeted
  to VRM with vrm-c/bvh2vrma (MIT). The 100STYLE Dataset - Ian Mason, CC BY 4.0 (https://zenodo.org/records/8127870).
- `walk-neutral.vrma`: her walk, one stride (frames 584 to 666) of the 100STYLE Neutral forward-walk take, with its
  root motion. `turn-left.vrma` and `turn-right.vrma`: two stepped turns from the same take (frames 4829 to 4914, and
  3221 to 3306). Retargeted with vrm-c/bvh2vrma (MIT). The 100STYLE Dataset - Ian Mason, CC BY 4.0
  (https://zenodo.org/records/8127870).
