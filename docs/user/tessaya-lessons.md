# Lessons

Short lessons in Help, each a few steps that end in something working. A step ticks once you have
used the page it teaches, on any of your devices. Each step names its feature in
[Everything Tessaya does](tessaya-features.md).

## Your first ten minutes

<!-- id: first | for: new | minutes: 10 -->

Talk to her, dictate one line, hand her one job. Three wins, then you know the shape of everything.

1. **Say hello.** Say her name and ask anything; the pill wakes and she answers. Say "Hey Tessaya, what can you do?" <!-- wake -->
2. **Dictate one line.** Click in any text box, hold Alt+Win, say a sentence, let go. Say "Lunch at one, new line, bring the charger" <!-- dictation -->
3. **Hand her one job.** Ask for something that takes a few minutes; she plans it first. Say "Start a job to sort my downloads folder" <!-- jobs -->
4. **Say yes.** Nothing runs without your yes. Read what it would do, then approve it. Say "Yes, go ahead" <!-- approvals -->

## Set up your Tessel

<!-- id: tessel | for: home | minutes: 8 -->

Pair it, wake it, make the buttons yours.

1. **Pair it.** Plug it in and add it with the digits on its eyes. Say "Add a Tessel" <!-- tessel-setup -->
2. **Wake it by name.** Stand anywhere in the room and call her. Say "Hey Tessaya, how's your battery?" <!-- wake -->
3. **Pick your buttons.** Choose what a double or long press does. Say "Make a double press of S1 start music" <!-- tessel-buttons -->

## Make dictation sound like you

<!-- id: yours | for: maker | minutes: 6 -->

Style per app, a snippet, and rewriting what you have selected.

1. **Set your style.** Casual in chat, careful in email. Say "Be more casual in Slack" <!-- style -->
2. **Make a snippet.** One cue for text you type every day. Say "Make a snippet called my address" <!-- snippets -->
3. **Rewrite a selection.** Select text anywhere and ask. Say "Make this shorter" <!-- transforms -->

## Living together

<!-- id: home | for: home | minutes: 7 -->

Add the people you live with, so she knows who is speaking and keeps each person's things apart.

1. **Invite someone.** Each person gets their own memory and their own yes. Say "Invite Sara to the household" <!-- household -->
2. **Teach her your voices.** A short guided read for each person. Say "Learn my voice" <!-- voiceid -->
3. **Send a message home.** Messages are off until you turn them on. Say "Tell Sara dinner is ready" <!-- intercom -->

## Let her work for you

<!-- id: work | for: builder | minutes: 9 -->

From "what's this error?" to jobs that run while you are away, and what she may do alone.

1. **Ask about your screen.** Press the screen shortcut and ask. Say "What's this error?" <!-- screen -->
2. **Steer a session.** Talk to an agent session without switching windows. Say "Tell the build session to run the tests" <!-- sessions -->
3. **Choose what runs alone.** Trust decides what still asks you first. Say "Always ask before sending email" <!-- trust -->
