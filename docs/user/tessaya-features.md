# Everything Tessaya does

One short page per feature: what it is for, what to say, and where it lives. Tessaya's **Help** reads this page, so
the same words show in her window, in the browser and in her own answers. The longer guides are linked from each one.

## Use Help

Press **Ctrl+K** in Tessaya's window and type what you want to do. Pages, settings and these features come up as you
type; pick one with Enter. Type a question instead ("how do I insert my address?") and choose **Ask Tessaya**: she
answers from this page and says which feature it came from. If nothing here covers it, she says so rather than
guessing. **F1** opens Help beside the page you are on, already showing what that page is for. In a browser, Help is
under Settings → Tessaya → Help. You can always just ask her out loud: "how do I make a snippet?"

**Lessons** in Help walk you through a few steps each. A step ticks itself once you have used the
page it teaches, on any device: a step done from your phone shows as done on your computer.

Each feature says **Live** (on your devices now), **Beta** (works, still changing) or **Coming** (planned, not here
yet).

## Dictation

<!-- id: dictation | task: type by speaking | status: live | area: talk | place: dictation | on: desktop, web, voice | for: new | guide: tessaya-dictation.md#start -->

Speak and your words are typed into whatever app you are in.

Try saying: "start dictating" · "stop dictating"

1. Click in any text box.
2. Hold **Alt+Win**, speak, and let go. Your words are typed where the cursor is.
3. Press **Esc** before you let go to throw it away.

Good to know: Recognition runs on this computer by default, and nothing you say leaves it.
